A little more about
breathe.

A calm, teen-focused space for information, reflection and small self-care activities.

WHY IT EXISTS

A softer place to start.

breathe. is designed to make general mental wellbeing information feel approachable rather than clinical. It gives you somewhere to learn, pause, reflect and find ideas for reaching out.

It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional care.

PRIVACY

Your reflection stays on your device.

In this version, journal and check-in information is saved locally in your browser. It is not sent to a breathe. server. Anyone who can access the same browser profile or device may still be able to see locally saved information, so use a device you feel comfortable using.
