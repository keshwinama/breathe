Understand how you
feel.

Feelings can be complicated. Learning what they can look like may make them a little easier to understand.

〰

Stress

Stress is a natural response to difficult or demanding situations.

You might notice tension, difficulty relaxing, trouble concentrating or changes in sleep when life feels demanding.

Small pauses, breaking a task into steps and talking to someone you trust can be useful places to start.

○

Anxiety

Everyone feels worried sometimes. Anxiety can involve strong or ongoing worry or fear.

Feeling anxious sometimes is part of being human. When worry becomes intense, ongoing or starts getting in the way of everyday life, it can be helpful to talk to a trusted adult or professional.

♡

Low Mood

People can have days when they feel sad, tired, empty or less interested in things.

Mood can change for lots of reasons. If low mood lasts, feels difficult to manage or starts affecting school, relationships, sleep or everyday activities, reaching out for support can help.

☾

Sleep

Sleep can affect energy, concentration and general wellbeing.

A regular wind-down routine can make it easier to give your mind a break. Keeping a consistent sleep schedule and making some time to relax before bed may be useful.

✦

Understanding Feelings

Feelings can change and can be influenced by many different things.

You do not have to find the perfect word for how you feel. Try noticing what happened, what you are feeling, and what you might need right now.

Common Questions

Simple answers to questions about everyday wellbeing.

Do I need to know what is wrong? No. You can ask for support even when you are not sure what you are feeling.

Does breathe. diagnose me? No. This website provides general information and self-care ideas, not diagnosis.

Why talking about mental wellbeing matters
