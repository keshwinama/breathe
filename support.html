You don't have to
handle everything alone.

Support can start with one small conversation. You can choose who feels safest to talk to.

♡

A friend

You can say that you have been having a difficult time and would like someone to listen. You do not need to explain everything.

⌂

A trusted adult

This could be a parent, relative, teacher, coach or another adult you trust.

✦

Someone at school

A counsellor, pastoral lead, nurse or another member of staff may be able to listen and help you find your next step.

○

A professional

A doctor or mental health professional can help you understand what is happening and discuss support options.
